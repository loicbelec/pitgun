The thermal controller constructor explicitly selects V9 with no V2 driver
experiment. The game's governed preparation assigns those four V2 IDs when an
opponent policy is present. Their presence in the catalog/frontend is therefore
insufficient: the target physical/runtime profile must explicitly select the
right schema. This does not assert that every deployed model follows V9; older
driver-control/timeline workloads have their own supported schema and evidence.

Another issue precedes fitting: legacy aggressiveness increases requested
utilization **and decreases** control-error amplitude. That is not an explicit
performance/accident tradeoff. Small seeded utilization loss is not a driving
fault, and no incident/DNF/VSC law is implemented by this audit. V2 consistency
and tire-management arithmetic exists in separate paths; thermalV2 rejects the
historical correction-heat proxy and needs accounted integration.
