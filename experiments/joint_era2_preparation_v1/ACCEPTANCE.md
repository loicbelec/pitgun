checks, equal common equipment, exact source bridges and18exact fresh full-result
repeats. Any failure prevents development acceptance.

Against the selected nine, report the fastest reference in the common pool:

- `free`: all four proposals;
- `zero_extra`: A=2 and K=1, i.e. no discretionary aero/cooling points;
- `heavy_80`: >=80% of discretionary points on chassis+engine, where available.

At actual exit cap24, free must win at least one of seven and have median rank<=3.

at most five of seven (at least two contexts resist the shortcut). Free raw time

and circuit-dependent choices are desirable.

All gates are conjunctive. A failure means **REFINE**, not coefficient fitting or
hidden difficulty adjustment. This is bounded opened-seed development evidence;
no global-optimum, overall win-rate or fresh reserve claim. Freeze a complete
