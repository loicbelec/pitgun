# Conservative common fuel bridge — owner direction,28 November2026

Priority: open all five current eras on the revised simulation. Fine automatic
fuel estimation and player fuel/engine-economy controls are deferred. This direction
supersedes the earlier next-step recommendation to refine the estimator first.
Captured100/110kg failures, estimates and receipts remain unchanged evidence.

## Simple candidate policy

One explicit starting charge per era, common to player and all nine opponents,
independent of player setup, spending, driver identity, initial stint plan or race
result. Start with the same charge in practices and full races within that era:
no pre-session estimate or extra preparation simulation. Existing I/II qualification
remains at100kg. For III,160kg is an authored conservative **candidate to test**,
not a measured minimum, certified bound, regulatory value or identified tank size.
IV/V charges are selected and frozen during their own serial lots, not assumed
qualified from III. No live mass or curve changes are made by this document.

Fuel mass and consumption remain physically active: carrying excess mass costs
performance; heavier starts affect acceleration, tire work and energy use. All
cars get the same initial input, while consumption can legitimately differ with
driving and preparation. Never disable burn, refuel silently, scale consumption
coefficients or grant extra mass to individual troublesome setups.

## Qualification before field fitting

Screen actual full-distance III contexts, including regular calendars, invitations
and practice-accessible earlier circuits (SAOPAULO must be included in the next
corpus version). Cover actual entry V8 and developed V6T, representative low/mid/
optional caps, demanding power/aero/cooling choices, one/two-stop plans and high
commitment reference drivers. Freeze a finite screen before computing; retain
finish reserve, failures, input/binary/profile identities and exact repeats.

A finite empirical screen establishes its stated coverage, not a mathematical
promise that every imaginable legal configuration finishes. If it finds an
inadequate reserve, adjust the common IIIcandidate and recheck the fixed screen
before freezing the joint IA matrix. No per-driver or per-case patch. Operational
charge, finish-reserve target and any future capacity constraint remain distinct.
The physical calibration campaign must use the same declared charge that will
be used by the eventual local runtime. No post-fit mass substitution.

Store accepted charge in the new era profile and propagate it to native and WASM,
initial input identity, recovery/replay and verifier admission. Keep I/II profiles
and historical public artifacts immutable. A future revised charge has a new
identity and may require requalification; wall-clock/UI decisions cannot change it.

## Later gameplay/framework opportunity

Initial mass is already an explicit simulation input. A later controller may choose
a bounded start load and a deliberate power/economy mode with real pace, heat and
consumption consequences. Thermal protective derating remains a separate physical
response. This needs its own versioned controls, fuel-depletion outcomes and
acceptance campaign; no new slider or gameplay promise is introduced here.

Next: qualify the simple common IIIcharge, then joint fixed-nine/player preparation,
fresh reserve review and local admission; IV then V. Estimator sophistication is
no longer a prerequisite of this bridge. No public catalog or deployment now.
